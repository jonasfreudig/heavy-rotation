Accounts

Last.fmwhere your scrobbles go

Tap Connect, then Yes, allow access on the Last.fm page in your browser, and come back here.

  1. Create a free API account on Last.fm. Any application name and description will do; leave Callback URL empty.
  2. Copy the API key and the Shared secret it shows into these two fields.
  3. Tap Connect, then Yes, allow access in the browser, and come back here.

Allow access on the Last.fm page in your browser, then come back here.

Last.fm asks you to allow access again. Your waiting scrobbles are kept.

Discogsyour record collection

Your Discogs username is enough if your collection is public on Discogs (your privacy settings there).

Private collection? Connect with a token
  1. On Discogs, open Settings → Developers and tap Generate new token.
  2. Copy the token and paste it here. It gives this app read access to your collection.

TIDALexact links and playlists
  1. Open the TIDAL developer dashboard and log in with your TIDAL account. Accept the guidelines the first time.
  2. Create an app; any name will do, for example "Record Shelf".
  3. In the app's settings, add this redirect URI and switch on the scope playlists.write: recordshelf://tidal
  4. Copy the app's Client ID and Client Secret into these fields.

The TIDAL buttons now open the exact song, album or artist. To create playlists in your TIDAL, sign in once.

Look

Theme

Colors always follow your wallpaper (Material You).

Style

Tinted soft: calm raised shapes in the cover's color, the cover only glowing at the top. Soft glass: the cover blurred behind the whole page, frosted buttons over it.

Now playing

What plays, on the turntable or the phone: as a line at the top of the shelf, or as a record peeking out above the search button.

Background color

The shade of the tabs and the glow behind their top: the colors of what plays now (the record spinning or the song on the phone, else the last one you played), your wallpaper's colors, or none.

Scrobbling

Phone scrobblinginstead of Pano Scrobbler

Android calls the permission notification access. Record Shelf uses it only to see what music apps are playing; it doesn't read your notifications.

Switch greyed out with "Restricted setting"? Open App info, tap ⋮ at the top right, choose Allow restricted settings, then try again.

Pano Scrobbler is still installed. Turn off its scrobbling or uninstall it, or every track is scrobbled twice.
Apps

Apps show up here once they play something. Only apps switched on are scrobbled.

Length of tracks nobody knows

Used when Discogs, MusicBrainz and Last.fm all lack a track's length. Such tracks show as ≈3:30 on the record page.

Stats and listening

Listen on

Each button searches the service for that song, album or artist. Spotify opens in its app; the others open their web page, which their app may take over.

Tidy up

Videos, app sounds and other non-music scrobbles can be hidden from your stats. Nothing changes on Last.fm.

Your data

Backupto a new phone, or into a new version

One file with your history, hide list, activity, collection, tracklists, caches and settings. Keys, tokens and logins stay out of it: connect again after restoring.

Data from Discogs, Last.fm, Deezer, MusicBrainz and TIDAL; Record Shelf isn't affiliated with any of them. Your keys and tokens stay on this phone and are left out of backups. Track lengths come from Discogs, MusicBrainz and Last.fm.
